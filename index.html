What to wear

Dress Code

A little colour guide for every celebration, so you can plan your outfits with ease.

🌼

Haldi

Bright / Yellow

Wear: Yellow, mustard, light cotton

Avoid: Heavy silks & dark colours

🌿

Mehendi

Green / Festive

Wear: Greens, pastels, light lehengas

Avoid: Very dark or heavy outfits

🎶

Sangeet

Festive / Glam

Wear: Sequins, jewel tones, easy-to-dance outfits

Avoid: Outfits that restrict movement

💍

Wedding

Traditional / Ethnic

Wear: Sarees, lehengas, sherwanis, kurtas

Avoid: White & black

✨

Reception

Formal / Elegant

Wear: Gowns, suits, indo-western, sarees

Avoid: Casual wear
